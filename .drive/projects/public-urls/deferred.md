# Deferred

- Alchemy `Prisma.Website`: a site with a custom domain cannot be deployed with a token limited to one project, or by a deployer without `workspaceManage` access, which the released site allowed. Every domain ownership route needs that access, including the two reads the RoutingUrl makes (the verification record with `dns`, and the workspace's domain ownerships on a hostname change). Fixing it needs an `ownership: false` form that sends `createDomainOwnership: true`, plus those reads tolerating a refusal, or Prisma opening them to project tokens. Will, 2026-11-26: "we don't need to support it yet". Not built now; revisit when a project-scoped deployer needs a site with a custom domain. Design: `slices/u2-routing-url-resource/website-domain-design.md`.
- Alchemy `Prisma.Project` (`Project.ts`, the two creates) and `Prisma.Database` (`Database.ts`, the create): they treat every 409 on create as "already exists". Since the 2026-11-26 spec, a 409 on `POST /v1/projects`, `POST /v1/databases` and `POST /v1/projects/{projectId}/databases` can also be `quota-exceeded`, the workspace's database limit. A user at the limit gets a wrong message after the retries, such as "already exists but is not visible yet". Found by the system-design review of 18b1b158; outside the public-URLs slice. Fixing it needs the quota code typed in Distilled (a patch giving it its own error), then the two resources telling it apart.
